== Strategy ==
Merryweather is a very powerful Hero, preventing Maleficent from playing [[Curse]]s to her location (though not from [[Move an Item or Ally|moving them]] there). Try to play her on top of one of Maleficent's Move an Item or Ally locations, to prevent Maleficent from circumventing Merryweather's effect.
