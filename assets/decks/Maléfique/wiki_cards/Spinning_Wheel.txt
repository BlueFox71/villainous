== Clarifications ==
* If [[Dreamless Sleep]] is at the same location, the amount of Power gained is reduced by 2 since the Strength taken into account is the Strength the [[Hero]] has at the moment of defeat, not the Strength it has once it's in the discard pile.

== Strategy ==
Spinning Wheel can produce a hefty amount of Power over the course of the game, and pays for itself after a single [[Vanquish]] action or [[Dragon Form]] play. Try to [[Move an Item or Ally|move it]] to locations with high-Strength [[Hero]]es, particularly ones with [[Sword of Truth]] attached.
