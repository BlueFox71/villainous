== Strategy ==
A generic, strong Ally. Great for taking out stronger [[Hero]]es, especially those with [[Sword of Truth]] attached, but using these to defeat [[Guards]] is usually a waste. However, if you're trying to find your [[Curse]]s, and you don't have any Heroes in your Realm, feel free to just [[discard fodder|discard this]].
