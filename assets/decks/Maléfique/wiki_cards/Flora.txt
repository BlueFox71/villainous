== Strategy ==
Forcing Maleficent to play with her hand visible makes it a lot easier to play against her. If you know she has no [[Curse]]s in her hand, you can focus more on your own win condition, and not have to worry about slowing her down. Flora also lets you know if Maleficent has any [[Condition]]s in hand, letting you know if you need to play around them. She's a solid pick, though relatively easy to get rid of, and unable to discard a Curse on play. Try to play Flora to locations without any [[Ally|Allies]].
