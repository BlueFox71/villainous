== Clarifications ==
* If King Stefan is played during Maleficent's turn (e.g., via [[Deception]]), but after she has already moved herself, Maleficent can no longer play actions at her previous location, but can play all actions at her new location.

== Strategy ==
Possibly the most powerful card in Maleficent's Fate deck, as King Stefan can potentially discard two [[Curse]]s when played, taking out a [[Forest of Thorns]] on his own, and moving Maleficent into a [[Green Fire]]. If no Green Fire is on the board, Maleficent can at least be moved to make her next turn be at a suboptimal location.
