== Strategy ==
The hardest Curse to get rid of, since Maleficent can easily avoid its discard condition by simply [[Move an Item or Ally|moving]] [[Ally|Allies]] into Dreamless Sleep's location, instead of playing them directly there. Only one [[Hero]], [[Fauna]], can discard this when played to its location. It also has the handy ongoing ability of decreasing the Strength of Heroes at its location, making [[Vanquish]]ing easier, though keep in mind that allowing Heroes to be at a Curse's location makes that Curse vulnerable to [[Once Upon a Dream]].
