== Strategy ==
A solid Hero, as she can discard [[Forest of Thorns]], and has about a two-thirds chance to bring out another Hero. If [[King Stefan]] is that Hero, there's a chance for three [[Curse]]s to be discarded in one [[Fate]] [[action]], making for a devastating combo.
