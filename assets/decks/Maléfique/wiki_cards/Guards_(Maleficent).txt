'''Guards''' is a [[Hero]] in [[Maleficent|Maleficent's]] [[Fate deck]].

== Strategy ==
The strength of Guards is that they require multiple [[Ally|Allies]] to get rid of, which can make them rather annoying. However, they are vulnerable to [[Dragon Form]], and cannot remove established [[Curse]]s. Other Heroes should be prioritized over Guards if possible.
