== Strategy ==
A very useful effect if you have a [[Hero]] of the right Strength in your Realm, but Malice is not the easiest Condition to trigger. Unless an opponent currently has a Hero of the right Strength in their own Realm, it's probably best to just [[discard fodder|discard this]], and use [[Dragon Form]] or a [[Vanquish]] action to deal with your Hero.

Of your possible opponents:
* [[Davy Jones]]/[[Jafar]]/[[Madam Mim]]/[[Scar]]/[[Ursula]] - have a strong enough Hero that they ''must'' defeat, but doing so doesn't immediately win them the game
* [[Captain Hook]]/[[Evil Queen]]/[[Pete]]/[[Ratigan]]/[[Shere Khan]] - have a strong enough Hero that prevents them from winning, but won't necessarily be played (or must have their Strength increased)
* [[Cruella De Vil]]/[[Gaston]]/[[King Candy]]/[[Mother Gothel]]/[[Prince John]]/[[Queen of Hearts]]- have a strong, obnoxious Hero that doesn't need to be defeated, but probably will be
* [[Dr. Facilier]]/[[Hades]]/[[Syndrome]]/[[Tamatoa]] - have a strong, obnoxious Hero, but their Strength can be reduced
* [[Horned King]]/[[Lady Tremaine]]/[[Lotso]]/[[Yzma]] - can completely ignore strong enough Heroes
* [[Oogie Boogie]] - has one strong enough Hero that he ''must'' defeat (although his strength can be reduced), but doing so will win him the game.
