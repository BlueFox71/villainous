== Strategy ==
One of the weaker Heroes, King Hubert's main use is to protect [[Flora]] and [[Merryweather]], or any Hero with [[Sword of Truth]] attached, as Maleficent cannot (barring the [[Raven]]) [[Vanquish]] and [[Move an Item or Ally]] on the same turn. If no such other Hero is in the Realm, the other Fate pick might be of more use. A niche use for him could be to move Raven into a less optimal position.
