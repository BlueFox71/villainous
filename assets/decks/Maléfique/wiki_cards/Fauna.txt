* If both copies of [[Dreamless Sleep]] are at the same [[location]], Fauna only discards one of them if she is played there.

== Strategy ==
The only Hero that can discard Dreamless Sleep when played. She's a great pick if that [[Curse]] is out, a dud if it isn't.
