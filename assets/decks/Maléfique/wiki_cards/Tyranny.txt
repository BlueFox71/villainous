== Strategy ==
The effect of this card can be quite useful, letting you get to your [[Curse]]s, but the [[Ability]] for the Condition to trigger is very sensitive to which [[Villain]] you're facing as an opponent:
* [[Captain Hook]]/[[Davy Jones]]/[[Gaston]]/[[Madam Mim]]/[[Scar]]: Very likely to have multiple [[Ally|Allies]] out to defeat [[Hero]]es
* [[Horned King]]/[[Pete]]/[[Queen of Hearts]]: Must have at least ''four'' Allies out in order to win
* [[Jafar]]: Can have Heroes as Allies due to [[Hypnotize]], in addition to his conventional Allies, so more likely to trigger this later in the game
* [[Dr. Facilier]]/[[King Candy]]/[[Mother Gothel]]/[[Oogie Boogie]]/[[Prince John]]/[[Shere Khan]]/[[Syndrome]]/[[Tamatoa]]: Has many Allies in their deck
* [[Hades]]/[[Lotso]]/[[Ratigan]]/[[Yzma]]: Has at least four Allies, but usually won't have many in play
* [[Lady Tremaine]]: Can have up to three Allies in play, but can refrain from playing all of them
* [[Cruella De Vil]]/[[Ursula]]: only has ''two'' Allies in her Villain deck
* [[Evil Queen]]: only has ''one'' Ally in her Villain deck
Against Captain Hook, Pete, Queen of Hearts, or Scar, Tyranny is a solid card to hold on to, and will most likely trigger. Jafar, Dr. Facilier, Mother Gothel, Prince John, and Syndrome are a bit more hit or miss, but can still often trigger. Never keep Tyranny if you're only playing against Cruella De Vil, Ursula, or Evil Queen.
