== Strategy ==
The highest-Strength Hero in Maleficent's Fate deck, Prince Phillip's main use is taking out the [[Raven]], though discarding a Goon or three is pretty nice, too. He can also protect [[Flora]] or [[Merryweather]] from being [[Vanquish]]ed.
